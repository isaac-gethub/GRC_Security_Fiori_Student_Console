Why this career exists

Every SAP landscape is one role away from a real problem.

It is not a niche skill with uncertain demand. It exists because of how SAP access works, because the business keeps changing, and because the next five years add work rather than remove it.

One over-broad role is enough

A single over-broad role can create a segregation-of-duties conflict. A single unreviewed emergency session can become an incident nobody can explain afterwards. Managing that is continuous work, for as long as the system runs.

Access risk doesn't hold still

A ruleset scoped correctly last year can go quietly blind after a Fiori migration. A role split that closes one gap can open another. The business keeps changing underneath the access model.

The next five years make it more valuable

Landscapes are moving to the cloud and starting to grant access to automation and AI agents the way they grant it to people — so every ruleset, role design and access review has more to account for, not less.

How every lab works

Built like a real ticket — judged like a real reviewer would.

See a full sample lab and the method →

Every lab follows the same structure, so you learn a method you can repeat on any client system.

  1. The ticket. A realistic request or incident, with the business process behind it and why it matters.
  2. Objective and design. What a good outcome looks like, and the approach a senior consultant would take.
  3. Scope. Exactly what is in scope, where the data comes from, and the check that stops you scoping it wrong.
  4. Execution. A three- or four-part walkthrough, with what to capture as evidence at every step.
  5. Verification. How you prove the fix works — not just that it looks finished.
  6. Pushback and escalation. The challenge a manager, auditor or business owner will raise, with a model answer to compare against yours.
What we do and don't claim
  • Lab steps are written for a live S/4HANA and GRC training system, using the ZZ_TRAIN_ practice namespace — never production objects.
  • Business Role Management, User Access Review and cloud identity (BTP, IAS, IPS, IAG) labs run as design and simulation exercises.
  • Completing the labs builds practical skill and interview readiness; it is not the same as production project experience, and we don't describe it that way.
  • TIB Systems is an independent training provider. This course is not endorsed by SAP SE.
What you'll be capable of

By the time you finish, you'll be able to:

  • Diagnose why a ruleset, role or workflow stopped doing what it was built to do — not just notice that it did.
  • Design and build the fix yourself — a corrected function definition, a role split at the right authorization-object boundary, a redesigned approval path.
  • Configure GRC Access Control, PFCG roles, SU24 defaults, MSMP/BRF+ workflows and Fiori Launchpad spaces in your own practice namespace.
  • Take a real support ticket — a locked-out user, a broken approval, a role that's too broad — and resolve it the way a production support consultant would.
  • Advanced: run a mass load, make a go/no-go call, work a hypercare queue, govern cloud identity and answer an external auditor's requests.
  • Tell a correctly scoped role from one that only looks correct, so you don't close a business process along with the gap.
  • Defend a design decision under live, unscripted pushback from a business owner, a developer or an auditor.

Questions

Before you enrol

Who is this for?

Junior and mid-level SAP consultants, auditors and administrators moving into SAP Security and GRC, and anyone who has the theory but not the hands-on judgement employers test for.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You need to understand that SAP access is controlled through configuration and roles that can be inspected and changed — the Foundations modules teach exactly that.

Where should I start?

Complete Foundations 1–12 at your own pace before Intermediate session 1. Intermediate then works Tracks A–B (design, then build); Advanced works Tracks C–D plus the cloud identity, BRM, UAR and privileged-access labs.

What does it cost, and how do the levels work?

Intermediate: Design & Build is $950; Advanced: Go-Live, Support & Cloud is $850; both together are $1,650. Each is a one-time payment with its own certificate. Advanced starts a week after each Intermediate cohort ends.

Can I go straight to Advanced?

Yes, if you already design and build SAP roles and GRC configuration at work. We confirm it with a short entry check before you enrol.

How much time should I plan each week?

About 12 hours: 8 hours of live class (four 2-hour sessions) plus repeating the lab steps on your own login and your self-study labs.

How is this different from a video course?

It is taught by a live instructor. Every lab is a scenario you work through and write up, ending with the pushback and escalation questions real stakeholders ask — so you practise the reasoning, not just the clicks.

Will I get real hands-on practice, or just reading?

Both, in that order. You diagnose the ticket first, watch the instructor work it live on the S/4HANA and GRC training system, then do it yourself in your own ZZ_TRAIN_ practice namespace — never in production.

Is interview preparation really included?

It's built into the method: every lab ends with a stakeholder pushback and an escalation question, answered before you see the model answer, and each level ends with a stakeholder panel.

What happens after I finish?

You keep your marked builds and deliverables to talk through in interviews, and a certificate for each level you complete.
