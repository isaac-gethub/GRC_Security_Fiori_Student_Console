This course pairs the Primary Lab (the instructor's full source content) with this Course Guide, your self-paced companion. Each of the eight Labs runs the same way: the instructor demonstrates the full Lab live on the S/4HANA training server — diagnosing the ticket, the process, and the impact before touching a transaction, then executing the hands-on steps — and you then repeat the same Lab from start to finish on your own, in your assigned practice namespace, before moving to the next one.

This is a show-and-tell course: you are not expected to guess at anything the instructor didn't already demonstrate. If a step in your independent run doesn't match what you saw demonstrated, stop and ask before improvising — the goal of this course is that by the end, you can execute every onboarding activity in the companion manual without needing to ask.

Practice namespace only. Every hands-on build happens in your assigned ZZ_TRAIN_ role, ruleset, or workflow copy — never in a production object.
